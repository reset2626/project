期末專題

專題內容架構

1     Title

Give a title to your project work, e.g., Process Diagnosis and Troubleshooting in the High-Tech In-
dustry, Demand/Price Forecasting and Capacity Planning in the Petrochemical Industry, Schedul-
ing Optimization in the Fastener Manufacturer, Queueing System Improvement in McDonald, etc.



2     Background and Motivation

2.1    Background

Describe the problem background or context. e.g., how/why the process diagnosis problem benefits
us? how the scheduling optimization change the society?


2.2    Motivation

Describe your motivation and why you choose this topic. Why this problem/decision is significant
to our society or important to us.


2.3    Problem Definition

Give one or two sentences to define your problem clearly. e.g., This study uses data science technique
to identify the key variables and classify the good and defective products through addressing data
imbalance problem. This study applies genetic algorithm to find the best scheduling under the
machine capability constraint.



3     Literature Review / Related Works

3.1    Overview of Related Studies

Briefly review key academic or industry studies related to your topic. Summarize the manufactur-
ing problem, methodology, data setting, and main findings to demonstrate your understanding of
existing approaches.


3.2    Methodological Comparison

If multiple studies are included, compare their assumptions, methods, and practical applicability.
Highlight major differences and suitability under different manufacturing contexts.

3.3   Connection to Your Project

Explain how the literature supports your project. Identify gaps or limitations in prior work and
clarify how your approach extends or differs from existing methods.



4     Methodology

Provide a clear research framework to describe your overall analysis flow. The framework may
include (but is not limited to) data preparation (e.g., missing value imputation, outlier detection,
data transformation), feature engineering or selection (e.g., principal component analysis, stepwise
selection, clustering), predictive modeling (e.g., regression, decision trees, neural networks), per-
formance evaluation, pattern interpretation, and decision optimization (e.g., linear programming),
followed by managerial insights and conclusions.

Clearly specify the main method(s) you adopt in your study (e.g., decision trees, clustering, neu-
ral networks, queueing theory, optimization models, etc.). Explain why the selected method is
appropriate for your problem setting and justify your choice from both theoretical and practical
perspectives (Method Justification).



5     Data Collection and Analysis Result (Video demo in the class
      presentation)

5.1   Data Collection

Describe the data source and how your get this data.


5.2   Analysis

Use the method introduced in Section 4 to analyze your work. Show your calculation/procedure
in details (e.g., Python/package). Please use table or figure to illustrate your numerical result if
possible (i.e., Visualization).


5.3   Interpretation

What’s the result or the best alternative you suggest? What’s the prediction accuracy? How to
support your decision-making based on the prediction results? What’s the risks you face when your
prediction is inaccurate? What’s the insight or story behind the pattern you found?

Summarize and guide the future research of your project work.



專題評分標準

    1. 貢獻與影響力（Contribution / Impact）：研究是否具有實務應用潛力、回應當前重要議
       題，或對後續研究產生推動作用。

    2. 方法論合理性（Methodology Justification）：是否清楚說明為何選擇該方法與實驗設計，
       包含理論依據、相關文獻支持，以及與替代方法之比較與說明。

    3. 表達與呈現（Presentation）：研究是否清楚、有條理地呈現，包含論文撰寫品質、圖表設
       計，以及口頭報告表現。

    4. 嚴謹性與技術品質（Soundness & Technical Quality）：方法設計、實作品質與實驗分析
       是否達到研究水準，並具可驗證性與再現性。

    5. 創新性（Novelty）：是否提出新的想法、概念或方法，而非僅重複既有研究成果。



專題注意事項

    • 資料與文獻引用：若使用開源資料、外部圖表、程式碼、模型或任何文獻內容，請務必提供
      完整且可追溯的引用資訊（作者、年份、期刊/會議或網址連結等），並依學術規範清楚標示
      來源。嚴禁抄襲、未標示出處或不當挪用他人成果。

    • AI 工具使用規範：本課程鼓勵同學適度使用 AI 工具作為思考輔助（例如：釐清觀念、協助
      除錯、改善表達、討論方法設計），但不接受將題目或專題需求直接複製貼上並產生整份成
      品。若有使用 AI 協助，歡迎同學在報告附錄或 README 中簡要說明 AI 的使用範圍與協
      作方式，或提供相關對話紀錄連結（非強制，但可酌予加分）。

    • 本專題重點在於 「從真實問題出發，轉化為可分析模型，並提供決策洞察」。
